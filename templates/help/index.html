🚀 Quick Start Guide

  1. Log in using your institutional email and password.
  2. Admin users are redirected to the Admin Console; Academic Staff go to the Staff Dashboard.
  3. Admins: Upload a CSV of student records via Import Data.
  4. The system auto-calculates each student's risk level after import.
  5. Staff: View the At-Risk Alerts panel to prioritise interventions.
  6. Open a student profile to see GPA trends, risk factors, and save advisor notes.
  7. Download a PDF report from the profile or the Cohort Report from the Admin Console.

📊 Understanding Risk Levels

🔴
High Risk
CGPA below the High Risk threshold, or failure in 2+ core courses. Requires immediate advisor intervention.
🟡
Medium Risk
CGPA in the medium-risk band, or failure in 1 core course. Monitoring and early intervention recommended.
🟢
Low Risk / Clear
Student is performing within acceptable academic standards. No immediate action required.

Academic Staff (Advisor)

  • Download cohort summary report
